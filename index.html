The Story

Rooted in Faith,
Forming disciples

Catechesis has been part of the Church since the earliest days of Christianity. Following the command of Jesus Christ to “go and make disciples of all nations,” the Church has always passed on the faith through teaching, witness, prayer, and community life.

As described in the Directory for Catechesis, catechesis is more than religious instruction—it is a process of forming disciples who grow in relationship with Christ and live the Gospel in daily life. From the first Christian communities to today’s parish classrooms, catechists have helped believers understand, celebrate, and live the faith.

This mission continues today as catechists guide children, youth, and adults to encounter Christ and grow as members of the Church.

The Church organizes the teaching of the faith around four foundational dimensions, often called the four pillars of the Catechism.

The Creed

Profession of faith, essential truths

Sacraments

Sacraments of faith

Commandments

Life of faith, moral conduct

Lord's Prayer

Prayer of the believer
